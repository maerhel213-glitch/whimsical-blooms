✦ A LITTLE FLOWER GUIDE ✦

Flower Facts.

Every flower carries its own beauty, story, and meaning. Discover little facts about the blooms in our garden.

01 · THE CLASSIC

Rose

Roses are one of the most widely recognized flowers. They are often associated with love, beauty, and affection.

🌹
02 · THE SUNNY ONE

Sunflower

Sunflowers are known for their bright yellow petals. Young sunflowers can turn toward the sunlight as they grow.

🌻
03 · THE ELEGANT BLOOM

Tulip

Tulips come in many colors and shapes. They are especially famous for their beautiful spring blooms.

🌷
04 · THE FRAGRANT ONE

Lavender

Lavender is known for its purple flowers and pleasant fragrance. It is also commonly grown as an ornamental plant.

🪻
05 · THE DELICATE BLOOM

Lily of the Valley

Lily of the valley produces small, bell-shaped white flowers. Despite its delicate appearance, the plant is toxic if eaten.

🤍
06 · THE SPRING BLOOM

Cherry Blossom

Cherry blossoms are famous for their soft pink or white petals. Their blooming season is often associated with the arrival of spring.

🌸
07 · THE SIMPLE BEAUTY

Daisy

Daisies usually have white petals surrounding a yellow center. They are often associated with innocence and simplicity.

🌼
08 · THE SPRING STAR

Daffodil

Daffodils are usually recognized by their bright yellow flowers and trumpet-shaped centers. They commonly bloom in spring.

🌼
09 · THE TROPICAL BLOOM

Hibiscus

Hibiscus flowers are large and colorful, making them popular ornamental plants in tropical and warm climates.
